=====================================================================
NYKAA MARKETING CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
End-to-End Data Analysis Project  |  Python + SQL + Power BI
=====================================================================

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------
Nykaa runs thousands of marketing campaigns across Email, SEO, Paid Ads,
Social Media and Influencer marketing, targeting different customer groups
in multiple languages. With 55,000+ campaigns, it is unclear which campaigns
generate profit, which lose money, and where the marketing budget should be
spent.

2. OBJECTIVE
------------
Analyse campaign performance (revenue, cost, ROI, funnel and engagement) to
find what drives profit and give data-backed recommendations to optimise the
marketing budget.

3. BUSINESS QUESTIONS
---------------------
1. What are the overall revenue, cost, profit and ROI?
2. Which campaign type, channel, language and target audience perform best?
3. Where do customers drop off in the funnel
   (Impressions -> Clicks -> Leads -> Conversions)?
4. How many campaigns are loss-making and what causes the losses?
5. How do revenue and profit trend month by month?
6. Does correct audience targeting or using multiple channels improve results?

4. DATASET
----------
File   : nykaa_campaign_data.csv
Size   : 55,555 rows x 16 columns
Period : July 2024 - June 2025 (June 2025 is a partial month, ends 24 June)

Column               Meaning
-------------------  -------------------------------------------------------
Campaign_ID          Unique campaign ID
Campaign_Type        Email, SEO, Paid Ads, Social Media, Influencer
Target_Audience      College Students, Youth, Working Women, Premium Shoppers,
                     Tier 2 City Customers
Customer_Segment     Same 5 groups as above (actual segment reached)
Duration             Campaign length in days (5 to 30)
Channel_Used         One or more of Google, WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram,
                     Email, Facebook
Impressions          Times the ad was shown
Clicks               Clicks received
Leads                Leads generated
Conversions          Customers who bought
Revenue              Revenue generated
Acquisition_Cost     Cost per ONE conversion (CAC)
ROI                  (Revenue - Total Cost) / Total Cost
Language             Hindi, English, Tamil, Bengali
Engagement_Score     Audience engagement score
Date                 Campaign date (dd-mm-yyyy)

7. KEY INSIGHTS
---------------
1. Overall: Revenue about Rs 2,866 Cr, cost about Rs 970 Cr, profit about
   Rs 1,896 Cr, overall ROI about 1.95.
2. 24% of campaigns (13,220) are loss-making (ROI below 0).
3. Acquisition cost (CAC) is the BIGGEST driver of ROI.
   Lowest 25% CAC campaigns: average ROI about 8.0
   Highest 25% CAC campaigns: average ROI about -0.32 (loss)
4. Campaign type, channel, language and audience all show very similar ROI
   (about 2.7), so none of them is a clear winner alone.
5. Only about 20% of campaigns have Target_Audience = Customer_Segment, and ROI
   is almost the same for match (2.75) and mismatch (2.70). Current targeting
   shows little effect.
6. Revenue is stable month to month (about Rs 250-260 Cr). June 2025 is an
   incomplete month.

8. RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------
1. Set a CAC cap: pause or rework campaigns whose acquisition cost is in the
   highest quartile (above about Rs 428 per conversion), they mostly lose money.
2. Shift budget to low-CAC campaigns and replicate their setup.
3. Review audience targeting: alignment does not currently improve ROI, so test
   sharper segmentation.
4. Run A/B tests on channel and language instead of assuming one is better.
5. Track CAC weekly on the dashboard to catch loss-making campaigns early.

9. LIMITATIONS
--------------
- June 2025 is a partial month, so the latest trend point is understated.
- Currency assumed to be INR.
- Campaign type, channel and language show very little variation, so the
  conclusions focus on CAC.
